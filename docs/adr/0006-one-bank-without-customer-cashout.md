The user clarified that all purchased, gifted, promotional and complimentary tokens enter one account bank without expiry and remain until spent on games. Players cannot request cash refunds or convert tokens back into money in the current product. This supersedes the ordinary purchase-refund eligibility/valuation discussion in Q43/Q48 and closes Q45's source-spending attribution branch: no FIFO/free-first policy, source-lot balances, game-to-grant consumption allocation or refund-reserved wallet balance is needed.

Keep order/grant/ledger provenance to authorize credit once and retain history; it does not create separate token banks. The accepted one-token game restoration for unopened or unrecoverable games still returns credit to the same bank. Unexpected provider refund/reversal facts must still be retained for Super Admin reconciliation, without introducing customer cash-out, normal purchase-refund APIs or automatic negative balances. Any future exceptional staff money action is separately scoped rather than inferred from provider capabilities.
